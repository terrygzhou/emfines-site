Our story

Two decades, one studio, and a simple standard.

EM Fine Studio began with a workbench and a belief: jewellery should be made for the person wearing it, not for a shelf. Over more than 20 years we've designed, built, and repaired thousands of pieces for families across Australia — and every one of them still carries our stamp.

We work in solid gold and sterling silver only. We don't outsource, and we don't cast generic stock. When you commission a piece, you're commissioning us — and we treat your story the way we'd treat our own.
