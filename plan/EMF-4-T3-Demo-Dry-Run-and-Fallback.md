## 3-Minute Demo Script (with timing)

### 0:00–0:30 — Opener: "What Emfines Does"
- "EM Fine Studio is an Australian jewellery design studio — 20+ years of experience in custom design and repairs."
- "We make one-of-a-kind pieces in solid gold and sterling silver. Every piece is a collaboration between you and the studio."
- *(Scroll to hero, point at headline + stats bar)*

### 0:30–1:15 — Walk the Site (Services → Work → Story)
- "This is the live site we just built. It's a single-page experience: services, our work, our story, and a direct enquiry path."
- "Click 'What we make' — custom design or repair/assessment. That's the two services, deliberately simple."
- *(Navigate: Services section → scroll to Our Work gallery)*
- "The work section shows real pieces. Clients come for the story, stay for the craftsmanship."
- *(Scroll to Our Story — keep it brief)*

### 1:15–2:00 — The Enquiry Flow (Live Demo)
- "The core of the site: the enquiry form. This is where a customer's interest becomes a commission."
- *(Open the form, fill in: name, email, interest = 'A custom design', brief = 'One-off ring, anniversary')*
- "Submit. The request goes straight to our inbox — no middleware, no CRM. We reply within two business days."
- "Invalid submissions get rejected at the form level. Clean UX, no dead ends."

### 2:00–2:30 — Close: What's Next for the Client
- "From here: we schedule a design consult, quote a budget range, and move to the design phase."
- "The whole flow — from first click to a booked commission — is under five minutes."

### 2:30–3:00 — Q&A Buffer
- "Happy to take questions — or I can walk through any section in more depth."
